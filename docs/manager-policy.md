## Commission questions and source rules

Sales Plan pages 3–4, sections 4–9:

1. New customer, incremental expansion, or renewal?
2. What is the actual contractual commitment?
3. What is the agreed collection frequency? Quote pricing does not prove annual commitment.
4. How many employees actually activated, and on what date?
5. Is an expansion baseline and incremental-only collection confirmed?
6. What is the management-approved share for a shared deal?
7. Is a documented exception, renewal rate or payout rule required?
8. What net eligible subscription was collected, refunded, and actually paid as commission?

Eligible subscription excludes VAT, implementation, hardware and other non-subscription charges. Explicitly eligible recurring extras may be included. Approved special incentives use a separately documented adjustment.

Standard new-customer bands are marginal 8%, 10%, 12%, 15% at 100%, 150%, 200% achievement; earlier bands are not repriced. Proportional allocation of a crossing deal's subscription value needs explicit confirmation because the supplied plan does not specify the detailed allocation method.

Annual commitment / annual upfront: full commission after full collection.
Annual commitment / monthly or quarterly: 50% after the first complete installment, remainder proportional to later eligible collections.
True monthly: three-month advance after the first complete monthly payment, then collections from month four.
Expansion: 5% of collected incremental eligible subscription, capped to the eligible first-year amount.
Refunds reduce net collections; previously paid commission remains visible and can produce a negative outstanding balance.

Renewal rates, semiannual payout and other contractual exceptions are not invented. Missing answers or approval leave entitlement unresolved, while preserving the win, quote, collection data and annual forecast.

## Periods and compensation

Target progress uses activated new-customer employees and activation dates. Commission uses the closing period's configured rules. Cross-period activation is flagged for a period-allocation decision rather than silently assuming eligibility.

Changing a saved quarter's rules recalculates that quarter; the editor warns about this. Changed standard rules require a documented approval note. New quarters do not copy a salary promise.

Existing records retain data and surface as requiring review when they lack the new explicit commission answers. Existing settings are normalized on read without overwriting stored rows or creating duplicate records.

## Daily manager and value evidence

Sales Plan sections 7–8: open deals should have real customer pain, a relevant stakeholder, a next action and date. Daily activity includes calls, conversations, discovery and demos; 20–30 targeted calls is contextual guidance, not a substitute for quality or approved meetings.

Cost of Inaction framework pages 4, 7–9 and 14–15: use customer-supplied measured/estimated evidence, avoid overlapping benefits, and leave strategic value qualitative. A scenario compares conservative addressable annual value with the first-year subtotal (subscription and one-time investment counted once). ROI and payback are scenarios, not guarantees.

Messaging framework pages 9, 17 and 19: guidance follows buyer persona and actual pain; it does not promise unverified savings, integrations or product capabilities.
